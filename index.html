          About Sprawl

          What is Sprawl?

          A free browser strategy game that reinvents tic-tac-toe: every turn you place a mark and expand the board by one square, so the grid keeps growing throughout the match.

          How do you play?

          Place your mark in any open square, then grow the board by one square edge-to-edge against any existing square. There are always exactly four empty squares, so the board never fills up — no draws by exhaustion.

          Is it free?

          Yes — no download, no signup, no account. Just open the page and play.

          Can I play a bot?

          Five difficulty tiers, Easy through Grandmaster, powered by an alpha-beta search engine that runs in a Web Worker so the page never freezes.

          Can I play online?

          Yes — quick match against a random opponent, or send a friend an invite link. Peer-to-peer WebRTC, no game server.

          Is there move analysis?

          A live evaluation panel with best line and alternatives, plus a post-game review with accuracy scores and move-quality badges.

          How to Play Sprawl

          1

          Starting Grid

          The match begins with 4 empty squares arranged in a compact 2×2 block.

          2

          Place Your Mark

          On your turn, place your mark (X or O) into any currently available empty square.

          3

          Expand the Board

          Immediately grow the board by adding one new empty square edge-to-edge against any existing cell.

          4

          Three in a Row

          Align 3 of your marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally anywhere on the grid to win!

          The Mathematical Twist: Since each turn fills one square and adds another, there are always exactly 4 empty squares on the board at any moment. The board grows, but the choice remains razor-sharp!
